HOW I WORK

four habits

UNDERSTAND THE PROBLEM FIRST

research, conversations and business context before any solution. what customers ask for isn't always the problem they need solved.

02

MAKE THE COMPLICATED CLEAR

03

CONNECT PLANNING WITH EXECUTION

strategy needs ownership, timelines, communication and follow through. i'm comfortable doing the thinking and the practical work.

04

KEEP LEARNING

i've worked across several industries because i'm interested in new businesses, people and problems.
